Program Overview

Grow Beyond Your Current Customers.

The Finger Lakes Procurement Initiative (FLX PI) is an eight-week procurement readiness program designed to help small businesses in Genesee County and western Monroe County become supplier-ready and successfully compete for purchasing opportunities with bigger companies, corporations, and government agencies.

Whether you're looking to diversify your customer base, increase revenue, or better understand how organizations purchase goods and services, this program will provide the knowledge, tools, and connections to help you compete with confidence.

Is This Program Right for You?

Built for Established Small Businesses Ready to Grow

This program is designed for established small businesses that are ready to grow by selling to larger organizations. Ideal participants include businesses in:

Manufacturing Construction & Skilled Trades Professional Services Information Technology Engineering Marketing & Creative Services Logistics & Transportation Facility & Maintenance Services Food & Beverage Office & Business Services Industrial Suppliers

You do not need previous procurement experience to apply.

Why Procurement?

Many large employers, healthcare systems, manufacturers, educational institutions, and public agencies are actively seeking qualified local suppliers.

The challenge isn't simply finding opportunities—it's knowing how to position your business to compete successfully. The Finger Lakes Procurement Initiative helps bridge that gap by preparing businesses to confidently pursue new opportunities and build lasting customer relationships.
